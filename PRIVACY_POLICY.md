# Privacy Policy — ScanDesk Pro

Last Updated: September 2026

### Zero-Telemetry Commitment
ScanDesk is engineered strictly as an offline-first, local-first utility.
- **No Cloud Tracking**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any user data.
- **Local Device Processing**: All barcode scanning, QR code generation, Wi-Fi decoding, and contact parsing happen directly on your local device.
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, provide an email, or connect to the internet.
- **Zero Third-Party Analytics**: No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry SDKs.
