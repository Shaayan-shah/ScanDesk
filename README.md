## 📱 How to Use on Your Mobile Phone

### 1. Instant Scanning via Camera
1. Open **[https://shaayan-shah.github.io/ScanDesk/dist/](https://shaayan-shah.github.io/ScanDesk/dist/)** on your phone (Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone).
2. Tap **"Allow"** when prompted for camera permissions.
3. Align any QR code or barcode inside the laser reticle — it decodes in milliseconds!
4. Tap **"🔄 Switch Lens"** to flip between front and rear cameras.

### 2. Scan from Photo Gallery / Screenshots
1. Tap **"Browse"** under the *Scan from Photo* card.
2. Select any high-resolution photo or screenshot from your phone gallery.
3. The app auto-scales the image and decodes the QR code instantly.

### 3. Install as a Native App Icon (Add to Home Screen)
- **On Android (Chrome)**: Tap the three dots `⋮` in the top-right ➔ tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
- **On iPhone (Safari)**: Tap the Share button `⎋` ➔ scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** `[+]`.
- You now have a standalone, full-screen native app icon on your phone!

## 🌟 Key Features

- 📷 **Real-Time Live Camera Scanner**: High-speed viewfinder with animated laser line and front/rear camera switcher.
- 🖼️ **Intelligent Gallery Decoder**: Auto-downscaling engine capable of decoding 48MP smartphone photos and screenshots in under 30ms.
- ✨ **Interactive Demo Samples**: 1-Tap test samples (Guest Wi-Fi, vCard Contact Card, Safe Web Link, EAN-13 Product Barcode).
- 🛠️ **QR Studio**: Real-time custom QR code generator (Wi-Fi, Contacts, URLs, Text, Phone) with 1-click PNG download.
- 📜 **Local Storage & History**: Private history with star favorites, notes, and search.
- 🔒 **Zero-Telemetry**: 100% offline-first architecture. No trackers, no ads, no cloud database requirements.
